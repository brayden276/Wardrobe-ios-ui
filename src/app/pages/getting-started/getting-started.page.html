A few useful things to know

How do I correct an item or hide it from suggestions?

Tap a garment in Wardrobe, then Edit to correct its details. The item menu also lets you archive it. Archived items are kept in your wardrobe but hidden from outfit suggestions; unarchive them from the same menu.

How do I find and manage my clothes?

Use Wardrobe's search and filters to narrow the list. Choose Select to manage several items at once. Outfits has its own search and Favourites filter.

When does the app use AI?

Adding photos and generating outfit suggestions use AI and need a connection. You'll be asked for AI consent before uploading photos or using AI Stylist. Manual Canvas lets you choose the pieces yourself; saved outfit images may still use AI.

Can I change my style preferences later?

Open Settings from Wardrobe or Analytics and edit Personal Style. You can also reopen this guide in Settings whenever you need it.
